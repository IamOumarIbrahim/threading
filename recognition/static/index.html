Consent

You are invited to take part in a research study on how people recognise objects in images made of thin lines.

  • Task: look at each image and type what you recognise.
  • Data: your answers, response times, device type and full name.
  • Use: answers are analysed under your username and may be published. Your name is kept only as a record of consent.
  • Your choice: taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time.

How it works

Each image is drawn only with thin lines. Enter what you can recognise.
